  Previous research in Philippine sentiment analysis has largely depended on traditional machine-learning approaches and manual annotation processes, resulting in smaller, less representative datasets. This study explores an emoji-based automatic annotation combined with transformer models to enhance sentiment analysis in Philippine social media.
